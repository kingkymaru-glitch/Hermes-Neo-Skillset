# I-Nation 2026 — pod requirements (transcribed from mranti.my/i-nation-2026)

Source: https://mranti.my/i-nation-2026
Event: 11 November 2026, Le Méridien Putrajaya.
Four closed-door Mission Labs; each seats 40–50 curated decision-makers with
authority to commit; organised as Pods (time-bound pilots, named lead, success
measure, stop-or-continue point).

---

## National Mission — Healthy Ageing

Bridging early detection, care transitions and referral pathways.
Anchor: insurers & takaful, healthcare operators & hospital groups, retirement &
senior-care providers, Ministry of Health.

**Pod 1 — Comprehensive Health Screening and Interventions**
- Tests: screening, risk stratification, personalised intervention plans, referral
  and follow-up as one integrated pathway.
- Malaysia context: 1 in 6 adults (15.6%) live with diabetes; NCDs cost RM64.2 bil/yr
  (4.2% of GDP); 2 in 5 adults skipped screening in the past year; 54% overweight,
  84% physically inactive.
- Baseline data to bring: existing screening programmes, risks identified,
  referral/follow-up rates, intervention outcomes and costs; clinical capacity;
  coverage, claims and funding models; target populations.
- Partners: healthcare providers, intervention/rehabilitation providers, insurers,
  healthcare financing partners, community and senior care organisations, government
  and research partners.

**Pod 2 — Transition Care (Tertiary to Home / Step Down)**
- Tests an integrated transition-care model from one private hospital to home or
  step-down care.
- Malaysia context: by 2040, 14.5% of population 65+; older-person healthcare
  utilisation projected RM21 billion by 2040.
- Baseline data: discharge volumes, readmission rates, discharge delays, home-care/
  nursing utilisation, care costs; discharge-planning capacity; funding models.
- Partners: healthcare and discharge-planning teams, home healthcare and nursing
  providers, insurers, healthcare financing partners.

**Pod 3 — Reverse Referral (Tertiary to Primary)**
- Tests a structured referral process to move stable patients to primary care.
- Malaysia context: public facilities handled 68.2M patient visits in 2023 (+18% over
  3 yrs); public hospitals took ~67% of admissions; outpatient no-show ~28%; wait 60 min
  (hospital) vs 41 min (primary clinics).
- Baseline data: specialist outpatient volumes, referral rates, waiting times, repeat
  visits, costs, readmissions; primary-care capacity; **digital referral/
  interoperability capability**.
- Partners: tertiary hospitals and specialist centres, primary care providers,
  insurers/takaful, healthcare financing partners, **HealthTech providers**,
  government and research partners.

## National Mission — Oil Palm Industry

Maximising yield, quality and sustainability through science-based solutions and new
value streams.
Anchor: plantation conglomerates, feedstock processors & refineries, MPOB & research
institutes, agritech & biotech firms, sustainability-linked financiers, Ministry of
Plantation & Commodities.

**Pod 1 — SawitElite: Breeding New-Generation Parental Lines**
- Problem: narrow genetic base (Deli Dura traced to 4 palms from 1848) → inbreeding
  depression. Land capped at ~6.5M ha, so yield gains must come from better planting
  material. Develop new maternal/paternal lines with "ideal palm" traits.
- Malaysia context: national yield 3.3 t/ha/yr vs 5 t/ha/yr target by 2050; 2nd-gen
  Angola/Nigeria Pop 12 lines at TRL 9 (MPOB-owned); gene editing (DNA-free
  CRISPR/RNP) proposed for non-abscission and virescens traits.
- Baseline data: FFB yield, oil-to-bunch, mesocarp-to-fruit, stalk length, harvest
  loss, FFA levels, Ganoderma incidence; breeding-programme performance.
- Partners: breeding/field-trial leads, research institutes holding germplasm,
  universities/biotech for genomics and gene-editing, multi-year funders.
- **LunaV fit: NO.**

**Pod 2 — SawitPure: Assuring Quality Planting Material**
- Problem: planting-material quality determines returns for 25+ years; smallholders
  have most to gain. Tenera yields 56–100% more oil than Dura/Pisifera. Add DNA-based
  quality assurance (SHELL DNA diagnostic) on top of MPOB's licensed seed system.
- Malaysia context: all seed producers MPOB-licensed; 5 MPOB-owned assays (4 at TRL 9:
  SureSawit Shell, SureSawit VIR, True-to-type, 50K SNP Eg50; Mantled at TRL 6).
  Mantled-free clonal palms yield 20–30% more oil.
- Baseline data: current QC method, turnaround time and cost per sample, annual
  seedling volumes, traceability records; yield records by material source.
- Partners: MPOB-licensed seed producers, MPOB, accredited labs, certification bodies,
  smallholders, replanting agencies, funders.
- **LunaV fit: NO.**

**Pod 3 — SawitDigit: A Connected Data Backbone from Nursery to Mill** ← TARGET
- Problem: labour shortages, limited mechanisation and low connectivity constrain
  digital adoption. Build a data-integration platform and AI tools for digital culling
  of off-types and ripeness detection (virescens colour change as a visual trigger for
  drone/imaging models).
- Malaysia context: architecture already defined — automated screening with genomic
  selection and AI, unified data platform, drone-based precision agriculture — but no
  published baseline accuracy or cost data yet; models still need training.
- Baseline data to bring: off-type/culling rates, labour per seedling, harvest
  intervals, ripeness grading, mill FFA/unripe-bunch rates, **imagery/sensor data,
  connectivity coverage**, labour cost per hectare.
- Partners: **AI, drone, robotics and agritech companies, connectivity providers,
  platform-development funders.**
- **LunaV fit: YES — this is the target pod.**

## Industry Mission — Low Altitude Economy

Building a resilient supply chain, a clear certification pathway, and disaster-response
capability for Malaysia's drone and advanced air mobility industry.
Anchor: drone OEMs & operators, telcos & infrastructure providers, logistics &
delivery platforms, CAAM, MITI/MIDA and disaster-management agencies.

**Pod 1 — Supply Chain Resilience, Local Capability and Thriving Industry**
- Problem: heavy reliance on imported technologies and proprietary systems → supply,
  export-control and cost risks. Strengthen local manufacturing, R&D, systems
  integration and technology transfer.
- Malaysia context: Malaysian Aerospace Industry Blueprint 2030 and NIMP 2030; existing
  capability in electronics, materials, batteries, software and automation.
- Baseline data: critical imported tech/components and origins; supply-chain risks and
  alternatives; existing local manufacturers/suppliers; local R&D and certification
  capability; demand and market potential.
- Partners: MITI, MIDA, MOSTI, NAICO; industry (Alphaswift, Aonic, Meraque, Autel
  Robotics, Kaizan Aero Manufacturing); universities/research institutes; drone
  operators and end users.
- **LunaV fit: NO** (hardware/manufacturing pod).

**Pod 2 — From Sandbox to Commercialisation: Regulatory & Certification Pathway**
- Problem: need a clearer, coordinated certification pathway to move eVTOL/AAM from
  sandbox testing to commercial deployment — airworthiness, operational approvals,
  airspace integration, safety assurance.
- Malaysia context: CAAM developing the proposed Malaysia LAE Framework; Special UAS
  Project pathway (CAD 6011 Part V) supports some higher-risk UAS/R&D operations.
- Baseline data: Malaysia LAE Framework status; eVTOL/AAM certification and airspace
  requirements; sandbox-to-commercialisation pathways; regulatory gaps and approval
  timelines.
- Partners: MOT, MOSTI, MIGHT, MIDA, CAAM, MCMC; MRANTI/AREA57, Futurise; NACSA, CSM;
  TCAB, eVTOL developers/manufacturers; SIRIM and research institutions.
- **LunaV fit: NO** (policy pod).

**Pod 3 — PTK2Dron: Disaster Preparedness and Emergency Response** ← TARGET
- Problem: PTK2Dron (established by MOSTI, coordinated by MRANTI) mobilises private-sector
  drone capability for disaster response. This pod explores scaling it into a structured,
  technology-enabled capability for rapid mobilisation.
- Malaysia context: activated for Selangor flood response Dec 2021 (20+ drones deployed
  for monitoring and supply delivery); 20+ companies/agencies and 200+ drones identified;
  NADMA briefings 2024 covered steering, SOPs and funding with PDRM, JBPM, JUPEM, MOSTI
  and MRANTI.
- Case for change: move from periodic activation to a predictable, scalable deployment
  model covering operator readiness, approvals, command, **data-sharing and analytics**
  for flood monitoring, SAR and emergency supply delivery.
- Baseline data: current operator/drone inventory, activation and approval process,
  deployment time and coverage, mission success rate, operating cost, existing SOPs.
- Partners: MKN; NADMA, JBPM, PDRM, APM; MOSTI, MRANTI; DEFTECH and industry; CAAM,
  MetMalaysia, JUPEM, MYSA; **UAS operators, telcos, universities**; state/district
  disaster authorities.
- **LunaV fit: YES — this is the target pod.**

## Industry Mission — Autonomous Mobility

Validating autonomous-vehicle operating models across controlled, public-transport,
urban and campus environments.
Anchor: demand owners & pilot anchors (MRANTI, Prasarana/Rapid KL, DBKL, universities),
AV & mobility solution providers, transport & safety regulators (JPJ, APAD, MIROS),
Ministry of Transport.

**Pod 1 — Controlled Innovation Park** — scheduled/on-demand autonomous service within
a controlled precinct; tests the full AV operating stack (route assessment, safety case,
remote supervision, incident escalation, maintenance, data reporting).
Malaysia context: MyAV testing route in Cyberjaya ~7.85 km.
Partners: site owner (MRANTI, Cyberview), AV operator, vehicle/robotics provider, MIROS,
JPJ, Futurise, MIMOS, insurer, university evaluation partner.

**Pod 2 — First- and Last-Mile Public Transport** — geo-fenced autonomous feeder
connecting with scheduled rail/bus services.
Malaysia context: Rapid Rail served 1.03M+ commuters daily as of Dec 2025.
Partners: Prasarana/Rapid KL, MARA Liner, APAD, MOT, JPJ, MIROS, local authority, AV
operator, TnG/KiplePay, insurer.

**Pod 3 — Urban and Municipal Precinct** — clearly defined low-speed autonomous service
for a specific municipal mobility need.
Partners: DBKL, PLANMalaysia, JKR, MOT, JPJ, MIROS, PDRM, Futurise, AV operator,
**mapping/C-ITS provider**, insurer.

**Pod 4 — University and Skills Campus** — autonomous campus service; campuses offer
stable users, repeatable routes and research partners.
Partners: university operator, student/accessibility representatives, AV provider, MIROS,
JPJ, Futurise, MIMOS, research teams, insurer.

**LunaV fit: NO** — all four pods are about running autonomous vehicles. The only thin
hook is Pod 3's "mapping/C-ITS provider" line (R-Tessera/CDM as the geospatial layer);
we would be a supplier to someone else's pod, not a lead.

## SISTEM — how MRANTI governs mission execution

System for Innovation & Scalable Technology-Enabled Missions. Two tracks:
- **National Mission Initiative** — Healthy Ageing & Oil Palm Industry: top-down policy,
  driven by national public interest, ministry KPIs and RMK13 mandates; led by a Ministry
  Mission Owner with corporate co-developers.
- Industry Mission Initiative — Low Altitude Economy & Autonomous Mobility (per the page's
  grouping).

Held in conjunction with the **GTSA Global Sandbox Forum 2026** — ASEAN partners exploring
interoperable testing frameworks, regulatory alignment and cross-border technology
validation.
