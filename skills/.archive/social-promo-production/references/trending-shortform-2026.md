# Trending Short-Form Patterns (2026, condensed)

Sources reviewed: Deftsoft "Go Viral on Reels 2026", Miraflow "How to Go Viral 2026",
Quora synthesis, motorcycle-creator YouTube tutorials.

## What wins
- **First 3 seconds = everything.** Weak hook kills reach regardless of rest. Scroll-stopping visual OR bold statement before audio matters.
- **80%+ watch with sound off.** Text overlay on frame 1 is mandatory — hook must read without audio.
- **Saves + shares > likes** now (algorithm weight shifted). Make it worth bookmarking / sharing to a rider friend.
- **Trending audio** in first 48h of a sound rising. Pick a rising phonk/electronic beat, not a dead one.
- **Vertical 9:16, <15-30s, completion-rate optimized.** One clear concept, not a brochure.
- **Visual hook > text hook.** Show the payoff (grip, lean, product) up front, explain after.
- **TikTok:** trend formats, duets/stitches get boosted. **IG Reels:** aesthetic + shareability. Early 30-min engagement decides reach.
- **Emotion = sharing trigger.** Speed, confidence, status ("your bike deserves this").

## Hook line bank (swap the opener)
- "YOUR TYRES ARE LYING TO YOU."
- "This is why you low-sided." (then show the fix)
- "168mph-rated. Road-legal. Backed by Petronas."
- "Cheap tyres cost more than this."
- "Your cornering grip ends HERE." (point at old tyre)
